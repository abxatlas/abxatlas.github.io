Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026. This policy applies to the Go Narrow app (also listed as “Go Narrow: Antibiotics Game”) on Google Play and the App Store.

Who is responsible

Go Narrow is made by Johanna Galaski, Germany, who is the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: coverthebug@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting SDK and no tracking. It does not send any personal or device data to the developer or to anyone else, and it makes no network requests while you play.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (stars, scores, daily results, study topics, review plates and settings) is stored only on your device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Contacting support

If you email support, for example with the error report button in the app, your email app sends your message, email address and anything you include to the address above. The error report only pre-fills the app version. Your message is used only to answer you and to fix the content. It is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (answering your enquiry), is not shared, and is deleted when it is no longer needed for that purpose. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), which processes messages on the developer’s behalf.

App stores

When you download the app, Google Play or the App Store processes data such as your account and device details under their own privacy policies. If you have agreed to share usage and diagnostics with Google or Apple, they may give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash rates. These do not identify you.

Who data is shared with

  • No data from the app is shared with anyone.
  • Support emails are stored with the email provider named above.
  • Links in the app (for example to EUCAST, WHO and IDSA) open those websites in your browser, where their own privacy policies apply.
  • If you share a daily result, it goes through your phone’s share sheet to the app you choose.

Data is never sold and never used for advertising.

Children

The app is made for medical students and health professionals and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

The app itself holds no personal data about you. For support emails you have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR); write to the address above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one in the German state where the developer lives (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg).

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data online (for example an optional leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
